Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 15, 2026

Legends Bot is a private tool operated by a single person (the "owner") to publish videos to the owner's own YouTube channel, Legends After Dark. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

Data the app accesses

  • An OAuth token that the owner granted for the owner's own YouTube channel.
  • The name and ID of that channel, and the view and like counts of the videos the app uploaded.

What we do not do

The app does not collect, store, sell or share personal data of viewers or of any other Google user. It does not access any Google account other than the owner's.

Storage and retention

The OAuth token is stored only as an encrypted secret in the owner's private code repository. Nothing else is stored. The owner can delete it at any time.

YouTube API Services

This application uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's privacy policy is available at https://policies.google.com/privacy.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact

Contact the owner through the About page of the YouTube channel Legends After Dark.
